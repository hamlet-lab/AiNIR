## Review expectations

Changes to Trust Gate semantics, evidence promotion, capabilities, effects, transaction rules, TrustReceipt, registry migration, or consumer profiles require an explicit invariant review. Routine documentation and isolated fixture additions may use a lighter review when they do not change decision behavior.
